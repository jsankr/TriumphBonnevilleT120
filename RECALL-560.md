# Safety Recall 560 / NHTSA 19V-133 — clutch cable / main harness

## What the US recall says
NHTSA campaign 19V-133 lists certain 2016–2019 Triumph Bonneville T120 and T120 Black motorcycles. Triumph reported that the clutch cable could contact the main harness cover and damage wiring, potentially causing loss of electrical power. NHTSA describes possible headlight/indicator malfunction or engine stalling, increasing crash risk. The stated remedy was dealer replacement of the original securing guide with an updated guide, at no charge under the US campaign.

Primary source: [NHTSA acknowledgement letter, 19V-133](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/rcl/2019/RCAK-19V133-9937.pdf). Additional regulatory record: [Part 573 report](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/rcl/2019/RCLRPT-19V133-4691.PDF).

## Critical market and VIN limitation
The campaign is a US recall record. The model-year range does **not** establish that every 2018 T120 in India is affected. The North American VIN ranges in the related Safety Recall Action Notice are not to be applied to an India-market VIN. Ask an authorised Triumph dealer in India to check the complete VIN against the applicable local recall/service campaign database and confirm whether the remedy has already been completed.

Do not attempt to improvise a cable guide or reroute the clutch cable based solely on this summary. If wiring damage, intermittent lighting, electrical failure or engine stalling is observed, stop riding and arrange inspection by a qualified Triumph technician.

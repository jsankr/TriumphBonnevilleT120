# 2018 T120 brake evidence

Triumph MY18 Bonneville family manufacturer brochure: Triumph MY18 Bonneville family specifications (manufacturer brochure): https://www.triumph-motorcycles.com.vn/data/categories/897/TRM_10759398_MY18Classics_Master_US_SP.pdf

- Front: twin 310 mm discs, two Nissin two-piston floating calipers, ABS.
- Rear: single 255 mm disc, one Nissin two-piston floating caliper, ABS.
- This differs from current-generation Triumph T120 web specifications, which list Brembo front calipers; current model specifications must not be applied to 2018.
- No verified 2018-VIN-specific brake mounting fastener torque, tool size, pad part number or removal procedure is established by this brochure. These remain unverified in the data.
- Existing 3D geometry is illustrative, not validated against the original Nissin caliper CAD. Do not treat the visualization as an accurate fastener-level exploded view.

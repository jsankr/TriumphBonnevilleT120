# Data notes — 14 November 2026

## Provenance and limitations
- Selected torque values are transcribed from Triumph Owner’s Handbook **3855663-EN issue 1 (August 2019)**, which covers Bonneville T120 models. It is not the 2018 factory workshop manual. Confirm exact applicability for the 2018 bike, market and VIN before use. See [`SOURCES.md`](SOURCES.md).
- Every component in `parts.json` now has a `torqueEvidence` status. Handbook references are distinguished from fasteners requiring workshop-manual verification.
- No front axle, fork pinch, brake caliper, brake disc, steering or structural torque is populated as a numeric value.
- No exact OEM part numbers or live India retail prices are asserted. Catalogue lookups require VIN/market/variant confirmation; prices require a dated dealer/supplier quote.

## Handbook torque references (not 2018 workshop-manual verified)
- Battery terminals: 4.5 Nm
- Chain adjuster lock nuts: 20 Nm
- Chain guard: 9 Nm
- Clutch lever nut: 3.5 Nm
- Oil filter: 10 Nm
- Spark plug: 12 Nm
- Sump plug: 25 Nm
- Rear wheel spindle nut: 110 Nm

## Maintenance schedule references
The 2019 handbook schedule lists chain slack check/adjustment daily, chain wear check every 800 km, and chain lubrication every 300 km. Lubricate after wet riding or when the chain appears dry. This is the handbook schedule, not an independently reconstructed 2018 interval.

Select a part, choose Place part marker, then tap its actual location in the uploaded photograph. Marker locations are session-only until exported.

The export contains view/stage coordinates and part IDs only—not photos, filenames, URLs or private vehicle identifiers.

Private fitment boundary

The website does not receive, contain, save, or display any full vehicle identifier. VIN-specific OEM fitment remains unverified until checked privately against an authorized catalogue. Do not infer exact part numbers from model year alone.
